okbrain keeps one person's knowledge in plain markdown, versioned in git, and
derives everything else — the search index, the graph — from the bundle. The
database is a cache; the files are the truth.

It stores notes in the [Open Knowledge Format](/concepts/okf.md), treats the
links between them as a [knowledge graph](/concepts/knowledge-graph.md), and
runs fully offline in the [local-first](/concepts/local-first.md) tradition.
